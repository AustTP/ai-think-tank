Centralized and decentralized data storage options have their pros and cons. Centralized options are cost-effective, scalable, secure, and reliable but may have vendor lock-in, single point of failure, and limited flexibility. Decentralized options are highly secure, flexible, autonomous, and cost-effective but may have complexity, limited scalability, and regulatory uncertainty. Blockchain applications and IoT devices are use cases for decentralized options, while enterprise applications and cloud computing are use cases for centralized options.
